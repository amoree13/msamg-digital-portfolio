### ✦ About This Activity

This activity focuses on applying presentation design principles to create a visually organized and engaging presentation.

**Free Coffee**

For my poster, I searched online and through social media for an image of Arrupe Hall that I could use as the background. I chose to include an image of the actual location to make the design more connected to the subject and to give the poster a clearer visual identity.

While creating this output, I found the design process challenging because I do not consider myself a naturally artistic person. With the limited time given, it was difficult for me to decide on the arrangement and overall appearance of the poster. However, I tried to apply basic presentation design principles throughout the process.

I considered **visual hierarchy** by making the important information more noticeable, allowing viewers to recognize the main details first. I also applied **contrast** to help the text and other elements stand out against the background image. Through the arrangement of the information, I tried to maintain **alignment and organization** so that the content would appear more structured and easier to follow. I also considered **proximity** by placing related information closer together to make the poster easier to understand.

Looking back at my work, there are still some elements that I would like to change or improve. However, this activity helped me understand that creating an effective design is not only about making something visually attractive, but also about properly organizing information and guiding the viewer's attention. Despite the challenges I experienced, I was able to complete the poster and gain a better understanding of how design principles can be applied to improve visual communication.

### ✦ About This Activity

**de Amor Closet**

For this activity, I created a brand called **de Amor Closet**. The name **“Amor”** comes from my own name, making the business concept feel more personal to me, while **“Closet”** represents the type of business that I imagined creating.

My concept for **de Amor Closet** is a small business that sells preloved items, such as clothes, jewelry, shoes, and other pieces that can be loved and used again. I wanted the brand to feel personal and welcoming while still having a clean and aesthetically pleasing appearance.

For my first design, I created the main logo and business name. The second design shows the logo applied to different combinations of my selected color palette. Lastly, the third design summarizes the overall color palette and typography that I used for the **de Amor Closet** branding.

### ✦ Color Palette

The colors I selected were **Brown (#9b4922), Pastel Gray Orange (#d8a790), Brown (#764b36), Light Orange (#efe7dd), and Orange (#d1774c).**

I chose these colors because I wanted **de Amor Closet** to have a clean, pretty, warm, and soft aesthetic that also reflects my personal style. The combination of brown and orange tones gives the brand a cozy and welcoming feeling, while the lighter colors help balance the palette and keep the overall design soft and clean.

I also felt that these colors matched the concept of a preloved business because they create a warm and timeless atmosphere, which fits the idea of giving previously owned items another chance to be loved and appreciated.

### ✦ Typography

For the typography, I selected **Sorts Mill Goudy, RoxboroughCF, and Italicized RoxboroughCF**.

I chose these fonts because I wanted the brand to look elegant while still feeling soft and approachable. The typography gives **de Amor Closet** a more personal and aesthetically pleasing identity. The different font styles also helped create variety and visual hierarchy within the design.

I used the fonts in different ways throughout the branding, with each one helping highlight important elements such as the logo, business name, headers, and tagline.

### ✦ Design Concept

The overall concept of my design was to create something that feels **clean, pretty, personal, and like me**.

One of the main highlights of my logo is the **cat**, because I love cats and wanted to include something personal that represents one of my interests. The cat adds a playful and unique touch to the branding while still matching the soft and aesthetic style that I wanted for **de Amor Closet**.

Overall, I wanted **de Amor Closet** to have a warm and welcoming identity that reflects both the concept of selling preloved items and my own personal aesthetic. Through the use of warm colors, elegant typography, and the cat element in the logo, I was able to create a brand identity that feels personal and meaningful to me.

### ✦ About This Activity

**Coping with Financial Blind Spots in Student Spending**

The main concept behind my infographic was to present the problem of student spending in a way that is easy to understand and relatable.

I wanted to show that financial difficulties are not always caused by large purchases. Sometimes, students may experience financial blind spots because they do not notice how much they are spending on small and frequent expenses.

The content of my infographic also presents a **proposed solution** to help students become more aware of their spending habits and manage their finances better.

One proposed solution is to use a simple financial tracking system where students can **log their transactions manually** and use **built-in formulas to automatically calculate totals and track budget limits**. This can help students monitor where their money goes and become more aware of their overall spending.

Another proposed solution is the concept of a **technology-enabled financial management application**. The application could capture expenses from **e-wallet and bank notifications**, while also allowing users to easily log cash transactions. It could automatically organize and monitor expenses while providing useful features such as **spending forecasts, budget alerts, and weekly summaries**.

Through these proposed solutions, the goal is to help students gain a clearer understanding of their spending habits, avoid financial blind spots, and make better financial decisions.

### ✦ Concept

The main concept behind my infographic was to present the problem of student spending in a way that is easy to understand and relatable.

I wanted to show that financial difficulties are not always caused by large purchases. Sometimes, students may experience financial blind spots because they do not notice how much they are spending on small and frequent expenses.

For the proposed solution, I developed the concept of a technology-enabled financial tracking tool that could help students monitor their spending. The proposed tool would allow users to record transactions, categorize expenses, set budget limits, monitor their spending patterns, and receive alerts when they are approaching their budget.

The overall goal of the proposed solution is to help students become more aware of their financial habits and make better decisions when managing their money.

### ✦ Design Choices

For the design of my infographic, I chose **blue as the main color** because I wanted the overall presentation to have a more formal and organized appearance. I also used lighter shades and visual elements to prevent the design from appearing too heavy.

I included icons, diagrams, and visual sections to make the information easier to understand. Since the topic involves financial information and data, I wanted to avoid presenting too much information through long paragraphs. Instead, I organized the content into different sections and used visuals to help simplify the information.

I also considered **visual hierarchy** by highlighting important information, statistics, headings, and key points. The use of sections, spacing, and diagrams helped organize the infographic and guide the viewer through the information.

Overall, my design choices focused on making the infographic **informative, visually organized, and easy to understand**.

### ✦ Creative Process

To develop my infographic, I first identified the problem that I wanted to focus on, which was financial blind spots in student spending. I then researched information and statistics online to support the problem and provide relevant data for the infographic.

After gathering the information, I selected the most important points and simplified them so they could be presented clearly within a one-page format. I then organized the content into sections, including the problem, supporting information, proposed solution, how the solution works, and its expected impact.

I created the infographic using **Canva**. While designing it, I focused on organizing the information clearly and using diagrams and icons to improve its visual storytelling and understandability.

Through this activity, I learned that creating an infographic is not only about making information visually appealing. It also requires selecting relevant information, simplifying complex ideas, and organizing the content in a way that allows the audience to understand the message easily.
